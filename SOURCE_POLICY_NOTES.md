# Source and evidence notes

## Primary live source in this prototype

NCS public job search: https://www.ncs.gov.in/Pmis-jobList

The Playwright adapter is a collection mechanism, not proof of production authorization. Keep it subject to NCS access, robots, terms, rate limits and any future official API/feed.

## Maharashtra employment signal

Maharashtra Rojgar / Mahaswayam: https://rojgar.mahaswayam.gov.in/

It is represented as a separate expansion source so its evidence is not silently merged with NCS posting evidence.

## Curriculum / qualification context

- DGT CTS: https://dgt.gov.in/en/cts-details
- NCVET National Qualifications Register: https://ncvet.gov.in/national-qualifications-register/
- Maharashtra skill / district planning context: https://www.mahaswayam.gov.in/

## Evidence rule

Every important decision value should retain source, date, URL/document and evidence text where available.
